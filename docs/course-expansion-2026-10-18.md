# Four new Korean topic chapters

Added Family Connections, Fruit and Small Snacks, At the Table, and A Walk Outdoors. Each has six referenced word senses and four stages: word introduction, word-meaning recall, sentence exchanges, and consolidation. Total addition: 16 lessons and 156 authored questions; the course now contains 140 lessons.

Definitions were compared with the pinned cc-kedict YAML, and the reference index was regenerated with source checksum verification. Context notes distinguish food senses such as pear/apple and explain respectful family vocabulary. Examples are app-authored and remain pending editorial review. Family/food/living interests include the relevant chapters, with the existing Hangul and grammar prerequisites preserved.

Corrected chapter progress to include word recall instead of skipping it. Fallback learning routes now insert recall before exchanges even when a topic is outside the learner’s selected interests. New recall cards accept explicitly listed equivalents such as grandma/grapes while rejecting other senses such as boat for the fruit lesson’s pear. Progress and labels now derive their total from the four actual stages. Existing completed lesson IDs are unchanged; completing three stages no longer implies the uncompleted recall lesson was done.

Validation: test curriculum uniqueness, references, answer choices, prerequisite order, beginner gates, full progress persistence, speaking card inclusion, and the chapter-stage regression before publishing. No providers, billing, microphone processing, or account data changed.
