# Founder story and website voice finishing

The public homepage and About page now share the founder's personal motivation: learning Korean to connect better with family, frustration with paywalls and apps that did not fit their needs, and the goal of making practice accessible. The text frames this as personal experience, not a claim that every other app is poor. A quiet paper-style background and conversation shapes support the existing visual style; reduced-motion users do not get the mascot sway animation.

Recorded website audio uses the current Supertonic 3 presets (M1 Chris, F1 Jessica). Its export mode uses 16 generation steps and bounded volume finishing. Live generation stays at 8 steps. No subscription, cloud speech API, retraining, or change of voices was introduced. The rebuilt files use a new versioned hash so browsers load the changed audio. There is no promise that more generation steps make every pronunciation perfect; editorial listening remains valuable.

Validation: all 102 website clips were regenerated. With the same active-sample measurement, RMS ranged from 0.0581–0.1199 before and 0.1181–0.1200 afterward; exported peak was at most 0.8912. This measures signal consistency, not perceived naturalness. The two regenerated hello samples transcribed as 안녕하세요. Both production builds, 80 app tests, and 11 speech-server tests pass. Browser verified homepage playback, the story section, and mobile width without overflow. Superseded generated files were removed only after verifying they belonged to the managed audio folder and were absent from the active catalog.
